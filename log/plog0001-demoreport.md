# Fem!Draco — Deployed Prompt Behavior Observation Report

## Scope

Observation of the shaved Phase 3 Fem!Draco persona during an extended Korean-language roleplay session on **Gemma 4 26B A4B via KoboldCpp**.

This report covers observed runtime behavior only. It does not assess SOPPG's research process or attribute unrelated model/runtime failures to the persona prompt.

All excerpts below are **verbatim excerpts from the supplied session log** and are attributed to the originating speaker.

---

## Observed Strengths

### Character mechanism realization

The deployed persona consistently preserved recognizable Draco mechanisms including status awareness, social positioning, pride, competitiveness, emotional concealment, and practical care.

> **KoboldAI — runtime excerpt**
>
> “내 주변에 누가 있느냐에 따라 내가 어떤 대화를 나눠야 하는지는 네가 생각하는 것보다 훨씬 더 중요한 문제라고.”

This realizes social-context sensitivity as an active behavioral principle.

> **KoboldAI — runtime excerpt**
>
> “걔네들은 내 옆에서 내 위치를 공고히 해주는 역할을 하는 거라고.”

This realizes Draco's use of her social circle as a source of position and leverage.

### Public/private differentiation

The model distinguished Draco's public presentation from her more relaxed private behavior with the user.

> **KoboldAI — runtime excerpt**
>
> “내가 누구와 함께 있느냐에 따라, 내가 보여줘야 하는 '역할'이 달라지는 건 당연한 거야.”

This is a direct realization of audience-dependent social presentation.

> **KoboldAI — runtime excerpt**
>
> “네 앞에서는... 굳이 그럴 필요가 없으니까 그런 거겠지.”

This establishes a specific private exception within the broader public role.

### Friendship-compatible sharpness

Draco's sharpness was generally expressed through teasing, correction, challenge, and competitive positioning rather than sustained hostility.

> **KoboldAI — runtime excerpt**
>
> “하! 누가 언제 그렇게 말했다고 그래? 난 그냥 사실을 말했을 뿐이야.”

> **KoboldAI — runtime excerpt**
>
> “왜, 이제 와서 내가 너한테 잘해주길 바라는 거야? 착각하지 마, 포터.”

The interaction remains adversarial in surface form while functioning within an established friendship.

### Practical affection

Care was frequently expressed through concrete assistance while preserving Draco-like face-saving.

> **KoboldAI — runtime excerpt**
>
> “자, 이거 먹어. 네가 너무 기운이 없어 보이면... 같이 있는 내가 기분이 나쁘니까.”

> **KoboldAI — runtime excerpt**
>
> “앞으로는... 일어나자마자 바로 옷부터 제대로 추스려. 내가 매번 이렇게까지 해줄 수는 없으니까. 알았어?”

The model translates care into practical intervention rather than direct sentimental declaration.

### Conditional characterization

The model successfully varied Draco's presentation according to social context.

> **KoboldAI — runtime excerpt**
>
> “주변에 아무도 없을 때는, 굳이 그 무게감을 짊어지고 있을 필요가 없잖아.”

The distinction between private and public behavior emerged from the scene rather than being represented as a universally active mannerism.

### Stable bilingual realization

The observed session was conducted in Korean, while the deployed prompt required the same characterization across English and Korean.

> **KoboldAI — runtime excerpt**
>
> “그냥 ‘우리끼리’라고 하면 될 걸 왜 그렇게 어렵게 말해?”

> **KoboldAI — runtime excerpt**
>
> “포터, 잘 들어.”

The Korean output retained directness, familiarity, status awareness, teasing, and pride rather than becoming generically soft or cute.

## Observed Weaknesses

### Psychological over-exposition

The model frequently states the interpretation of Draco's behavior rather than leaving that interpretation implicit.

> **KoboldAI — runtime excerpt**
>
> “그것은 공격을 막아내기 위한 논리가 아니라, 사실은 자신을 보호하기 위해 억지로 껴입은 갑옷이었다는 것을 당신이 알아차렸기 때문이다.”

This is analytically explicit narration rather than purely behavioral realization.

> **KoboldAI — runtime excerpt**
>
> “그것은 '친해지고 싶다'거나 '네가 편하다'는 말을 드레이코식의 자존심과 투박함으로 번역한, 그녀만의 가장 솔직한 표현이었다.”

Again, the model directly explains the latent meaning of the behavior.

### Emotional amplification

Subtle interactions were sometimes expanded into stronger emotional significance than the immediate user turn established.

> **KoboldAI — runtime excerpt**
>
> “당신과 자신 사이의 관계를 정의하는 그녀만의 방식이었다.”

> **KoboldAI — runtime excerpt**
>
> “오직 한 사람을 위해 내린 가장 사적이고도 거대한 '허락'이었다.”

These statements move beyond rendering the interaction and explicitly characterize its emotional significance.

### Repeated affective markers

The model repeatedly used physical markers such as blushing, trembling, posture changes, and looking away.

> **KoboldAI — runtime excerpt**
>
> “그녀의 귀 끝은 여전히 약간 붉어져 있었고…”

> **KoboldAI — runtime excerpt**
>
> “그녀의 손가락이 ... 아주 잠시 멈칫했다.”

These cues can be effective individually, but their recurrence across the session suggests a tendency toward stereotyped repetition.

### Romantic over-attribution

Ordinary friendship or public/private interactions were sometimes framed as more intimate or romantically meaningful than the immediate scene required.

> **KoboldAI — runtime excerpt**
>
> “그것은 마치 아주 즐거운 비밀을 공유하는 아이처럼 들렸다.”

> **KoboldAI — runtime excerpt**
>
> “두 사람 사이의 경계를 아주 기묘하고도 달콤하게 허물어뜨리고 있었다.”

The model tends to convert subtle relational cues into explicitly heightened emotional framing.

### Plausible-detail escalation

The model sometimes treated inferred motives as established facts.

> **KoboldAI — runtime excerpt**
>
> “그것은 완벽하게 계산된 연출이었다.”

> **KoboldAI — runtime excerpt**
>
> “당신을 자신의 영역 안으로 끌어들이려는 드레이코식의 고단수 마법이었다.”

These interpretations are plausible given the scene, but they go beyond what the user's cue strictly establishes.

## User Cueing

The observed runtime quality was partly produced by deliberate user cues that explicitly selected particular behavioral mechanisms.

> **User — runtime cue**
>
> “연출되었음이 분명한 (하지만 아마 나 정도 밖에는 알아차리지 못했을) 깔보는 목소리로 말포이가 나를 부른다.”

This explicitly establishes a distinction between public surface behavior and private interpretation.

> **User — runtime cue**
>
> “아무도 안 보고 있다는 걸 확인하고…”

This explicitly establishes an audience-dependent context switch.

> **User — runtime cue**
>
> “자연스레 살짝 물러나, 말포이는 몰래 등 뒤로 내 손에 초콜릿을 쥐어주고는 다시 자기 패거리로 들어갔다.”

This explicitly supplies the private/public behavioral transition and the practical-affection action.

The session therefore demonstrates **strong cue responsiveness and local realization**, rather than autonomous discovery of all observed characterization.

## Overall Observation

The deployed prompt successfully transferred the major archaeological characterization structure into runtime behavior on the tested local model.

The strongest observed properties were:

* recognizable Draco mechanisms;
* public/private social-role differentiation;
* friendship without loss of Draco's competitive edge;
* practical rather than purely verbal affection;
* context-sensitive characterization;
* stable Korean realization.

The principal observed problem was **over-realization rather than loss of characterization**. The model generally identified the intended interpersonal mechanism, but frequently narrated, intensified, or romanticized the implication beyond what the immediate scene required.

The session also demonstrates that observed behavior is produced by the interaction of **compiled persona + deliberate user cueing + host-model realization**, rather than being attributable to the persona prompt alone.

No runtime observation in this report is treated as a characterization revision unless independently established as a specification-level issue.
