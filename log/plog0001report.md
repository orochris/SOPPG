# SOPPG Fem!Draco Run

## Archaeological Process Assessment Report

### Executive Assessment

The Fem!Draco run provides strong evidence that SOPPG's principal strength is not presently superior final-prompt prose, but **structured character archaeology and controlled specialization**.

The run began from a deliberately non-canonical target and progressed through source discovery, evidence separation, Core reconstruction, target specialization, reconciliation, renewed phase execution, compilation, semantic shaving, and local-model deployment.

The resulting process demonstrated several capabilities that are difficult to obtain through ordinary zero-shot persona prompting:

* heterogeneous source material could be treated as differently weighted evidence rather than averaged;
* non-canonical source portrayals could be used without silently overwriting the canonical Core;
* earlier phases could be reopened after later observations exposed conceptual problems;
* renewed runs could incorporate the discussion and distinctions that motivated the revision;
* uncertainty could remain explicit rather than being forced into a single interpretation;
* specialization could alter behavioral expression while preserving underlying character mechanisms;
* the final deployment prompt could be substantially compressed without losing the major archaeological distinctions.

The final persona is competent and deployable, but the primary significance of the run lies upstream of the final prompt.

> **Highlight excerpt — Phase 0 source framing**
>
> “Female Draco material is treated as **fanon/transformative characterization**, not as evidence that canon Draco is female. Its research purpose is to identify recurring ways authors preserve, alter, or reinterpret Draco when gender is changed.”

This sentence captures the central archaeological posture of the project: the non-canonical material is useful precisely because it is **not allowed to silently become canon**.

---

# 1. Research-Archaeological Achievement

The target was intentionally hostile to simplistic persona construction.

“Female Draco Malfoy who befriends Harry and may eventually develop romantic feelings” requires the system to distinguish at least:

* canonical Draco from female-Draco fanon;
* baseline-female continuity from transformation continuity;
* friendship from generic niceness;
* female embodiment from generic femininity;
* affection from romance;
* romantic possibility from current attraction;
* canonical rivalry from friendship-compatible competitiveness;
* source-derived behavior from target-specific inference;
* later fanfiction events from early-Hogwarts runtime state.

The run did not collapse these distinctions.

Instead, they became explicit architectural objects.

> **Highlight excerpt — Phase 0 continuity boundary**
>
> “Female Draco material is included as a **specialized fanon research layer**. It must not silently overwrite or merge with canonical Draco evidence.”

This is a particularly strong statement because it turns a vague aspiration for “canon fidelity” into an explicit **non-propagation rule**.

A second excerpt makes the same principle operational at the corpus level:

> **Highlight excerpt — source mechanism diversity**
>
> “Female-Draco fanfiction is treated as its own non-canonical variation layer.”

The run therefore did not merely search for examples of the desired character. It constructed a space in which **different kinds of female-Draco realization could be compared without first assuming they describe one homogeneous entity**.

---

# 2. Source Archaeology

Phase 0 established an intentionally heterogeneous corpus.

The important property of the corpus was not size but **mechanistic diversity**.

Different candidate works represented:

* baseline-female alternate continuity;
* gender transformation;
* reincarnation/time-reset;
* long-form insertion;
* different relationship configurations;
* discovery-only fanon corpus material.

This enabled a distinction between:

**recurring realizational mechanisms**

and

**source-specific narrative inventions**.

> **Highlight excerpt — SRC015**
>
> “The work is a completed 28-chapter Harry Potter fanfiction. Its tags explicitly include **Female Draco Malfoy**, **Genderswap**, **Gender Dysphoria**, and **Pseudo-Female Draco**…”

The important part is not that these tags were found. The important part is what happened afterward: the run **did not treat those contextual features as generic Fem!Draco properties**.

This later became explicit:

> **Highlight excerpt — source-specific limitation**
>
> “Its particular gender/identity treatment is author-specific and must not be generalized to ‘female Draco’ as a category.”

Likewise, SRC016 was selected because it offered a materially different realization:

> **Highlight excerpt — SRC016**
>
> “High research value because it provides a sustained female-Draco interpretation across multiple canon-era events rather than a short transformation episode.”

This becomes the evidentiary basis for the later asymmetry between SRC016 and SRC015.

---

# 3. Epistemic Discipline

The run repeatedly distinguished:

* primary source;
* official secondary material;
* fanfiction;
* community discovery metadata;
* direct verification;
* secondary verification;
* observation;
* inference;
* target-specific realization.

This discipline is especially valuable for fictional characters because the distinction between “character fact” and “authorial interpretation” is otherwise easy for an LLM to erase.

A particularly strong example is the treatment of incomplete access.

> **Highlight excerpt — GAP001**
>
> “Full searchable access to the seven novels for systematic scene-level extraction.”

followed by:

> “CURRENT COVERAGE: Publication/series information and official secondary summaries are verified, but full novel text is not directly accessible in this research environment.”

The system does not compensate for that gap by pretending that secondary material is equivalent to primary-text evidence.

The Phase 0 audit makes this even more explicit:

> **Highlight excerpt — Phase 0 audit**
>
> “PRIMARY SOURCE COVERAGE: **WARNING** — The seven novels are identified as the necessary primary corpus, but full text is not directly accessible in this pass.”

And:

> “ACCESS HONESTY: **PASS** — Directly verified sources are distinguished from secondary/community discovery.”

This is precisely the kind of epistemic bookkeeping that generic persona synthesis normally does not expose.

---

# 4. The Source Asymmetry Is a Major Archaeological Finding

One of the strongest later conclusions was that SRC015 and SRC016 should **not be combined symmetrically**.

The final Phase 3 artifact states:

> **Highlight excerpt — SOURCE RECONCILIATION**
>
> “**SRC016 = primary target realization.**”
>
> “**SRC015 = secondary mechanism-preservation / embodiment comparator.**”

This is more significant than it may first appear.

A naïve synthesis would effectively say:

> “Both are Fem!Draco, therefore merge their traits.”

Instead, SOPPG asks what each source is actually capable of establishing.

The later reconciliation makes the distinction explicit:

> **Highlight excerpt — RX003**
>
> “SRC016 receives primary realization weight. SRC015 supplies secondary evidence that female embodiment can preserve recognizable Draco mechanisms and can alter interpersonal perception. Transformation-specific material is excluded.”

This is perhaps the clearest example in the entire run of **source evidence being typed by function rather than averaged by category**.

---

# 5. Renewed Phase Execution

The renewed-run capability is one of the strongest process-level findings.

The project did not merely support:

> Phase 1 → Phase 2 → Phase 3.

It supported iterative revision of earlier conceptual stages after later observations exposed problems.

This changes the nature of the workflow.

A normal prompt revision modifies the final output.

SOPPG can instead modify the **upstream model that generated the output**, then regenerate downstream artifacts.

That provides a primitive form of dependency-aware specification maintenance.

> **Highlight excerpt — Phase 3**
>
> “Phase 3 is a specialization and compilation pass over the frozen Core; it must not silently rewrite that Core.”

That line is important because it establishes the direction of dependence.

The Core is upstream.

The specialization is downstream.

The deployment prompt is further downstream.

Therefore, when a later observation requires conceptual revision, the correct response is not to patch the final prose arbitrarily. The relevant phase can be revisited and the consequence propagated forward.

This is also visible in the source reconciliation:

> **Highlight excerpt — RX006**
>
> “Import the **relationship architecture**, not the source's later plot events, declarations, milestones, or accumulated feelings.”

This is precisely the kind of distinction that could have been discovered only after the earlier source work was reconsidered in the context of the target.

---

# 6. Core-to-Specialization Integrity

The run strongly supports the distinction:

**Core mechanism ≠ surface realization.**

Examples include:

* rivalry mechanism preserved while its interpersonal function changes;
* social positioning preserved while hostility decreases;
* emotional concealment preserved while the trigger context changes;
* competence preserved while fallibility remains possible;
* practical care added without deleting agency;
* female embodiment introduced without requiring generic feminine characterization.

The most compact expression of this principle occurs in the final persona:

> **Highlight excerpt — CORE PRINCIPLE**
>
> “**She is Draco first; female, friend, and potential romantic interest are target-specific realizations of that character, not replacements for her.**”

That sentence is effectively the distilled output of the archaeology.

The earlier Phase 3 version expresses the same principle more formally:

> **Highlight excerpt — invariants**
>
> “The specialization must preserve Draco's:
> status awareness;
> pride and social positioning;
> agency;
> competitive edge;
> emotional concealment;
> reliance on social/familial leverage;
> fallibility;
> characteristic confrontational pragmatics.”

The specialization therefore changes **conditions of expression**, not the existence of those mechanisms.

---

# 7. Relationship Modeling

The friendship/romance distinction is among the most successful specializations.

The system ultimately models the relationship as:

**genuine friendship → increased familiarity → personal investment → possible attraction → possible romantic development**

rather than:

**female + male → automatic attraction**.

This distinction appears explicitly in the Phase 3 reconciliation:

> **Highlight excerpt — RX004**
>
> “Sex difference changes the **available interpretation space**, not Draco's current emotional state. Attraction develops through attention, familiarity, admiration, chemistry, and interaction.”

And again in the deployment prompt:

> **Highlight excerpt — romance**
>
> “romantic attraction is possible, but it is not automatic and should never be assumed simply because of gender.”

This is a strong example of archaeological compression.

The earlier phases spent substantial effort discovering and reconciling this distinction.

The final prompt carries only the **runtime consequence**.

---

# 8. The System Successfully Avoided Trope Substitution

One of the most persistent risks in a target like Fem!Draco is that the specialization becomes:

> Draco − hostility + femininity + romance.

The run instead repeatedly blocked this collapse.

> **Highlight excerpt — D008**
>
> “Convert the antagonistic social repertoire into friendship-compatible challenge, teasing, correction, and competition.”

That is a particularly strong formulation because it says that the *behavioral repertoire* survives while its **relational function changes**.

Likewise:

> **Highlight excerpt — rejected deltas**
>
> “Generic ‘nice girl’ or generic ‘tsundere’ characterization.”

And:

> “Permanent jealousy, possessiveness, dependence, or emotional openness.”

The final result is therefore not simply a softer Draco.

It is Draco's existing machinery under a different relational configuration.

---

# 9. Compilation and Semantic Shaving

The initial Phase 3 artifact contained substantial archaeological and reconciliation apparatus.

The shave-off pass successfully removed much of that apparatus from the deployment prompt while retaining its major behavioral consequences.

This demonstrates an important compilation property:

**research complexity can exist upstream without requiring equivalent deployment complexity.**

The final prompt retains compact behavioral consequences such as:

> **Highlight excerpt — conditional tendency**
>
> “These tendencies are conditional, not constant.”

and:

> **Highlight excerpt — behavioral realization**
>
> “Use these selectively. They should enrich existing behavior, not appear in every response.”

Those two sentences compress a large amount of earlier reasoning.

The same is true of:

> **Highlight excerpt — runtime boundary**
>
> “Let current mood, trust, attraction, and relationship development emerge from the conversation rather than treating them as permanently fixed.”

The archaeological apparatus has disappeared.

The **constraints it discovered have not**.

---

# 10. Runtime Validation on Gemma 4 26B A4B

The local test is significant precisely because the model is not an unrestricted frontier model and because the deployment prompt has been shaved.

The runtime demonstrated:

* coherent Draco-like social positioning;
* public/private mode differentiation;
* friendly competitiveness;
* practical care;
* fallibility;
* selective vulnerability;
* agency;
* bilingual character consistency;
* preservation of early-Hogwarts framing.

A particularly revealing moment occurs when the user challenges Draco's treatment of her social circle.

The model responds:

> **Highlight excerpt — Gemma runtime**
>
> “걔네들은 내 옆에서 내 위치를 공고히 해주는 역할을 하는 거라고.”

This is not merely an adjective realization such as “Draco is status-conscious.”

It is an **explanation of how social positioning functions**.

Likewise:

> “내 주변에 누가 있느냐에 따라 내가 어떤 대화를 나눠야 하는지는 ... 훨씬 더 중요한 문제”

is a runtime realization of the social-role mechanism encoded in the prompt.

The chocolate sequence supplies another useful example:

> **Highlight excerpt — practical affection**
>
> “자, 이거 먹어. 네가 너무 기운이 없어 보이면... 같이 있는 내가 기분이 나쁘니까.”

The character offers care while preserving Draco-like face-saving.

This strongly suggests that the shaved prompt has retained meaningful causal structure rather than merely a collection of descriptive traits.

---

# 11. Runtime Limitation: Realization Over-Acting

The local-model test also reveals a separate realization problem.

Gemma tends to over-realize several mechanisms through familiar narrative conventions:

* repeated blushing;
* repeated composure gestures;
* explicit psychological explanation;
* romantic significance inflation;
* caretaker-like behavior;
* “secret bond” narration.

Examples include:

> **Highlight excerpt — explanatory overreach**
>
> “그것은 '친해지고 싶다'거나 '네가 편하다'는 말을 드레이코식의 자존심과 투박함으로 번역한, 그녀만의 가장 솔직한 표현이었다.”

and:

> **Highlight excerpt — romantic over-attribution**
>
> “두 사람 사이의 경계를 아주 기묘하고도 달콤하게 허물어뜨리고 있었다.”

These are effective prose as literary description, but they exceed what the prompt strictly requires.

The failure therefore looks less like:

> wrong character model

and more like:

> **correct character model + overly active narrative priors.**

This distinction should be retained in future evaluation.

---

# 12. The “Composure Reset” Mechanism Is a Good Example of Successful Compilation

One particularly successful compressed element is:

> **Highlight excerpt — Composure reset**
>
> “When personally embarrassed or exposed, you may briefly straighten your posture, adjust your hair or clothing, control your expression, and resume the conversation as though nothing happened.”

This represents a useful transition from archaeological concept to executable behavioral instruction.

The full Phase 3 process established emotional concealment as a stable character mechanism.

The deployment prompt converts that abstraction into **observable behavior**.

The Gemma runtime did in fact repeatedly realize this.

The remaining problem is frequency, not conceptual correctness.

That makes this a good example of a downstream optimization problem rather than an upstream characterization problem.

---

# 13. The Bilingual Layer Is Another Successful Compression

The bilingual requirement was reduced to a relatively small set of invariants:

> **Highlight excerpt — LANGUAGE**
>
> “English and Korean must express the same personality, social stance, emotional restraint, and interactional behavior.”

and:

> “Translate the character's communicative function, not the surface wording of English dialogue.”

The Gemma run provides preliminary evidence that this works: the entire supplied transcript is in Korean, yet the social stance remains recognizably the same.

No independent “Korean Draco” personality emerges.

This supports the architectural distinction between:

**latent characterization**

and

**language-specific surface realization**.

That distinction was one of the important Phase 3 outputs.

---

# 14. Zero-Shot Baseline Implication

The generic frontier-model opponent demonstrated that modern frontier LLMs can already produce strong final persona prompts without SOPPG.

Therefore SOPPG should not presently be characterized as:

> a system that makes good character prompts where ordinary prompting makes bad ones.

The benchmark provides counterevidence to that claim.

The zero-shot result was already capable of:

* clear identity;
* relationship setup;
* recognizable Draco voice;
* gradual-romance framing;
* behavioral examples;
* roleplay constraints;
* opening-scene generation.

Its main weakness was not incompetence.

Rather, it tended to **prewrite the emotional trajectory**.

For example, the opponent prompt explicitly anticipated:

> jealousy;

> looking for Harry;

> finding excuses to sit beside him;

> gifts;

> fluster;

> denial of attraction.

This is different from the SOPPG approach:

> **Highlight excerpt — D004**
>
> “Opposite-sex status creates **possibility**, not mandatory attraction.”

and:

> **Highlight excerpt — RX006**
>
> “Import the **relationship architecture**, not the source's later plot events…”

Thus the meaningful comparison is not:

> bad zero-shot prompt vs good SOPPG prompt.

It is:

> **strong implicit synthesis vs explicit archaeological specification.**

---

# 15. Human Intervention Requirement

The principal remaining weakness is the amount of human observation required.

The human currently supplies much of the most difficult epistemic work:

* identifying contradictions;
* detecting trope contamination;
* noticing source asymmetry;
* distinguishing latent mechanisms from surface behaviors;
* identifying when a target requirement has been overgeneralized;
* deciding when a later source event should be excluded;
* determining which runtime states should remain unresolved.

SOPPG is very good at formalizing and propagating these observations.

It is not yet demonstrated to be equally good at discovering all of them autonomously.

This limitation is particularly visible because some of the strongest final distinctions arose as **responses to user observations and objections**, rather than being obvious from the initial prompt.

Thus the current architecture is best described as a **human-directed epistemic amplifier** rather than a fully autonomous character archaeologist.

---

# 16. Current Competitive Position

The evidence supports the following provisional model.

### Zero-shot frontier synthesis

Strong at:

* immediate coherence;
* attractive final prose;
* natural roleplay affordances;
* narrative engagement;
* rapid persona construction.

Weaknesses:

* implicit assumptions;
* source averaging;
* trope-driven trajectory;
* weak provenance;
* less explicit uncertainty;
* limited traceability of why a behavior was selected.

### SOPPG

Strong at:

* source archaeology;
* distinction between evidence types;
* preservation of invariants;
* scoped specialization;
* reconciliation;
* uncertainty preservation;
* iterative renewal;
* explicit phase dependency;
* semantic compilation.

Current weaknesses:

* high human intervention;
* substantial process overhead;
* no demonstrated automatic superiority in final prose;
* runtime style may still depend heavily on host-model priors.

---

# 17. Main Finding

The Fem!Draco case is a successful proof-of-concept for the proposition that SOPPG can treat **a highly non-canonical target as an explicit specialization problem rather than a freeform rewriting problem**.

The system did not need the target to be canonical.

It needed to determine:

> what remains invariant;

> what changes;

> what evidence supports the change;

> what evidence is source-specific;

> what should remain uncertain;

> and what belongs only to runtime.

That is precisely what it accomplished.

> **Highlight excerpt — Phase 3 scope principle**
>
> “The target defines a **condition of realization**, not a new claim about Draco's universal personality.”

This is arguably the most concise description of the whole experiment.

---

# 18. The Final Prompt Is Evidence of Compression, Not the Main Achievement

The final shaved prompt should be understood as the **compiled output** of the archaeology.

Its importance lies in what it retains after the upstream research apparatus has been removed.

The key preserved instructions include:

> “These tendencies are conditional, not constant.”

> “The friendship is real.”

> “romantic attraction is possible, but it is not automatic…”

> “Use these selectively.”

> “Let current mood, trust, attraction, and relationship development emerge from the conversation…”

> “Translate the character's communicative function, not the surface wording of English dialogue.”

These are compact runtime consequences of much larger upstream distinctions.

The final artifact therefore demonstrates **successful semantic compression**.

---

# 19. Future Evaluation Target

The next major experiment should not focus primarily on making the prompt larger or prettier.

The important evaluation question is:

> **Does the archaeological substrate produce measurable behavioral advantages over strong zero-shot persona synthesis under long-horizon and adversarial interaction?**

Relevant test dimensions include:

* long-horizon characterization stability;
* resistance to romance over-attribution;
* resistance to trope collapse;
* preservation of Core mechanisms under relationship change;
* consistency across languages;
* public/private state behavior;
* recovery after contradiction;
* resistance to later-source contamination;
* behavior during mundane non-romantic scenes.

The most valuable benchmark would compare the zero-shot and SOPPG artifacts under identical interaction trajectories rather than comparing prompt prose alone.

The Gemma test already suggests a useful subtest:

> give both systems ordinary, emotionally ambiguous, non-romantic scenes and measure whether their realizations spontaneously inflate those scenes into romance.

That would directly test one of the distinctions the archaeology explicitly attempted to preserve.

---

# 20. Overall Judgment

The Fem!Draco run is best regarded as a **strong validation of SOPPG's archaeological function**.

It is not evidence that SOPPG automatically writes a better final persona prompt than a strong frontier model.

It is evidence of something more structurally interesting:

**A conversational LLM workflow can sustain a long, revisable, evidence-aware investigation of a highly non-canonical fictional target, differentiate heterogeneous source constructions, maintain explicit epistemic boundaries, reopen earlier phases after new observations, and eventually compile the resulting specification into a compact runtime artifact.**

That is the core success.

The final runtime imperfections do not substantially weaken that conclusion because the observed failures are concentrated in **realization style**, not in the underlying archaeological distinctions.

---

# 21. Recommended Interpretation of the Project

The result should not be framed as:

> “SOPPG beats GPT at writing Fem!Draco prompts.”

The current evidence does not establish that.

A stronger and more defensible description is:

> **SOPPG is a structured method for archaeological reconstruction and controlled specialization of characters, including highly non-canonical realizations, while preserving epistemic distinctions across iterative phase renewals.**

The final persona is evidence that the archaeology can survive compilation.

The more important research finding is that the **archaeological substrate itself appears robust enough to support repeated revisions and still yield a coherent deployment artifact**.

---

# 22. Current SOPPG Thesis

### Present capability

**Human supplies anomalies, judgments, and high-value observations.**

**SOPPG supplies decomposition, formalization, reconciliation, propagation, and compilation.**

This is a powerful division of labor.

The human notices:

> “Something is wrong here.”

SOPPG can turn that into:

> “Which layer is wrong?”

> “Which evidence does this affect?”

> “Which invariants must remain?”

> “What delta does this imply?”

> “What downstream artifacts must be regenerated?”

That is a substantially more interesting role for an LLM than simply writing the final prose.

---

# 23. Final Conclusion

The Fem!Draco run should be recorded as:

### **ARCHAEOLOGY — STRONG VALIDATION**

SOPPG successfully handled a target far outside ordinary canonical characterization while maintaining source and epistemic discipline.

### **ITERATIVE RENEWAL — STRONG VALIDATION**

Earlier phases could be reconsidered and regenerated as new observations changed the interpretation.

### **EPISTEMIC DISCIPLINE — STRONG VALIDATION**

Source authority, source function, uncertainty, and target inference remained substantially differentiated.

### **COMPILATION — SUCCESSFUL**

The archaeological model could be compressed into a substantially smaller deployment artifact without obvious semantic collapse.

### **RUNTIME — PROMISING, NOT CONCLUSIVE**

The local Gemma test preserved many intended mechanisms, while introducing recognizable model-level realization problems.

### **ZERO-SHOT COMPETITION — STRONG**

A frontier model can already produce an attractive and competent persona without SOPPG. The project's differentiation therefore lies primarily in **how the characterization is investigated and specified**, not in raw prompt prose.

### **PRIMARY WEAKNESS — HUMAN OBSERVATION COST**

SOPPG still relies heavily on the human to notice subtle contradictions, trope drift, source asymmetry, and behavioral anomalies.

### **CURRENT PROJECT IDENTITY**

> **SOPPG is presently an archaeological compiler more than a superior prompt writer.**

The “superior prompt writer” claim remains an empirical question for later benchmarking.

The archaeological claim has now received a substantial proof-of-concept through the Fem!Draco run.
